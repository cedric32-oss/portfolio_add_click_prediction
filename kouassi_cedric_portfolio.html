Browsing & Device Insights

Desktop dominates click volumes overall, especially in Education (~295 clicks) which generates ~80% more clicks than Mobile. However, Social Media breaks this trend with Mobile generating the highest clicks (~35).

💡 Actionable takeaway: Route Social Media ad spend toward mobile platforms while reserving Desktop for Education and News.

Timing & Positioning Insights

Click volume spikes during Morning and Afternoon windows, specifically when ads are located in Bottom and Side placements. Contrary to popular belief, Top placement does not systematically yield highest conversion.

💡 Actionable takeaway: Re-allocate expensive top-banner budgets toward bottom/side placements during peak morning/afternoon hours.

Demographic Interactions

Female users exhibit higher overall engagement, peaking in the Afternoon (~100 clicks) and staying high at Night. Male and Non-Binary demographics show lower, steady click counts (20-40 range) across periods.

💡 Actionable takeaway: Demographic targeting must be dynamically adjusted relative to the hour of ad delivery.
